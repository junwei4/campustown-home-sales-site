Q1. Which city has the highest median sale price?

Median prices use valid sales from $10,000 to $5,000,000, inclusive.

Q2. Which city's mean is furthest above its median?

Uses all rows, with no price rule. Gap = (mean − median) ÷ median.

Q3. How do city mean-price ranks change with the price rule?

Compares all rows against valid prices from $10,000 to $5,000,000, inclusive. Rank 1 is the highest mean.

Q4. How did median sale prices change by campus area?

Percent change from 2024 to 2025, using valid prices and sale dates.

Q5. What is the median days on market by campus area?

Days on market is contract date minus list date; rows missing either date are excluded. Prices must be valid.

Q6. What is the annual property-tax burden by campus area?

Sum of eligible annual tax divided by sum of sale prices. Includes valid prices and tax greater than $0 but less than 10% of sale price.

Q7. For a selected bedroom count, which city has the lowest median sale price?

Uses exactly the selected number of bedrooms and valid prices.

Q8. For a selected price range, which city has the shortest median days on market?

Range endpoints are inclusive and are bounded by the valid-price scope ($10,000–$5,000,000). Rows need both list and contract dates.

Q9. How many sales were recorded in each month?

Uses all rows with a sale date, without a price rule.

Q10. What is the median distance to campus for qualifying sales?

Select a campus area, maximum budget, and minimum bedroom count. The price scope remains $10,000–$5,000,000, inclusive.
